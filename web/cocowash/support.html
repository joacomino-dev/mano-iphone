Need a hand?

Here’s what people ask most about CocoWash: access to your photos, getting something back, and purchases.

Lo que importa, se queda.

Nothing is deleted until you confirm it

Swiping only marks a photo. Every step can be undone.

  1. You mark

    Swipe left or tap “Delete”: the photo is marked, not deleted. “Undo” reverts your last decision.

  2. You review

    Tap “Finish” to reach “One last look”. Tap “Recover” on any photo, or “Recover all”.

  3. You confirm in Photos

    Only tapping “Delete” and accepting the Photos prompt deletes anything. Items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Favorites are never deleted.

Frequently asked questions

Photos and access

How do I give or change access to my photos?

CocoWash asks on the last card of the intro, with “Allow access to Photos”. To change it later:

iPhone Settings › Apps › CocoWash › Photos
On iOS 17: Settings › CocoWash › Photos.

I only see some of my photos. Why?

You probably chose “Limited access”, so CocoWash only sees the photos you picked. To add more, tap “Filters”, then “Choose more photos”. To let it see your whole library, change the permission in iPhone Settings to “Full access”.

Deleting and recovering

I already confirmed the deletion. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. In the Photos app, find the Recently Deleted album, choose the photo and tap “Recover”. If you use iCloud Photos, it comes back on all your devices.

I deleted photos but don’t have more free space

Deleted items still take up space while they’re in Recently Deleted. To free it now:

  1. Open the Photos app.
  2. Find the Recently Deleted album.
  3. Tap Select › Delete All.

CocoWash can’t empty that album for you: that decision is always yours.

What does “Later” do?

It sets the photo aside without deciding: it isn’t kept or marked for deletion. It waits at the end of the pile, and Filters › Later shows them all together.

Photos in iCloud

Why is a photo slow to load, or shown as a “light preview”?

If the original is only in iCloud, CocoWash downloads it when it reaches the pile and gets the next ones ready. Offline, or if the download fails, you’ll see “iCloud · light preview”: a smaller version you can still decide on. The download is retried when you’re back online.

Can I use less data?

Yes. In Filters › Settings › Photos in iCloud, turn on “Save iCloud data”: originals only download when you pin or zoom a photo.

Purchases and ads

How do I remove ads?

Filters › Support has two one-time purchases:

  • “A year without ads”: lasts a year from purchase and doesn’t renew.
  • “No ads, forever”: never expires.

“Buy me a coffee” is a tip: it doesn’t remove ads.

I changed iPhones or reinstalled the app. How do I get my purchase back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with and tap Filters › Support › Restore purchases. The coffee isn’t restored because it’s a tip.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request it at reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account.

Where do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Filters › Support › Ad privacy. If there’s no consent form in your region, that button opens CocoWash’s page in iPhone Settings. Tracking across apps is managed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. More in the privacy policy.

On Android

How do I give or change access on Android?

At the end of the intro, Android offers “Allow all” or “Select photos”. With “Select photos”, CocoWash works only on the ones you picked and lets you add more. To change it: Settings › Apps › CocoWash › Permissions › Photos and videos.

I deleted photos on Android. Where are they, and how do I free the space?

They stay in your gallery’s trash for 30 days, where you can restore them; after that, Android deletes them on its own. To free the space now, open your gallery app (Google Photos, Gallery…), go to Trash and empty it from its menu.

Why does Android ask about favorites?

Android asks for confirmation every time an app changes the favorites in your gallery. So it doesn’t interrupt you, CocoWash keeps them while you sort and sends them to your gallery together at the end of the round, with a single prompt.

Google Play purchases: restoring and refunds

Purchases belong to your Google account. On another phone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same account and tap Filters › Support › Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Google Play: how to request a refund.

Write to cocowash@joaquinmino.work. CocoWash is made by one person; we reply as soon as we can. To help us help you faster, tell us:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the CocoWash version (in Settings › General › iPhone Storage › CocoWash);
  • what you were doing and what you expected to happen.

There’s no need to send personal photos. If you attach a screenshot, cover anything you’d rather not share.
