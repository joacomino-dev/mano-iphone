In short

  • Your photos and videos are analyzed on your iPhone. CocoWash does not upload them for analysis or advertising. Sharing is always your choice.
  • No accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics of our own. Advertising SDKs process the data described below.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads. They’re only personalized if you give permission.
  • Apple processes payments. CocoWash never sees your payment details.

Who we are

The controller is Joaquin Miño, an independent developer based in Quito, Ecuador. For any privacy matter, write to cocowash@joaquinmino.work.

Your photos and videos

With your permission, CocoWash reads your Photos library to show it to you and help you sort it. Similarity, quality and text in screenshots are worked out on your iPhone with Apple’s own tools. Your photos, videos, their details and those results are not sent to the controller or to Google for analysis or advertising. The optional Apple place-name lookup and user-initiated sharing are described below.

The existing local classification can count detected faces as a signal when assessing an image. It does not identify people, build face-identity profiles or offer person-based grouping.

If you choose Share, the iOS share sheet sends only the media you select to the app or recipient you choose, under that service’s privacy terms. CocoWash does not share your library automatically.

If a photo’s original is in iCloud, your iPhone downloads it from your own iCloud account, just as the Photos app would.

Only what you confirm in Apple’s Photos prompt is deleted. Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and favorites can’t be marked for deletion. CocoWash’s suggestions (similar, blurry, screenshots) are just that: the decision is always yours.

Place names

To write the city next to a photo, CocoWash asks Apple’s maps service to turn its location into a place name. Only the coordinates are sent, rounded to about 1 km; never the photo. The name is saved on your iPhone so it isn’t asked for again.

If you turn off “Location” in Filters › Settings (under Photos and under Videos), CocoWash stops making these lookups. Apple handles them under its privacy policy.

What stays on your iPhone

CocoWash keeps on the iPhone itself your decisions (keep, later, marked for deletion), your settings, language, session progress, space freed, place names, and when the last ad was shown, so ads stay spaced out. None of it is sent anywhere. It’s removed when you delete the app and, like any app’s data, may be included in your iPhone backup.

Google AdMob ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. To show and measure them and to prevent fraud, Google’s SDK, built into the app, may process:

  • Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • technical device information such as model, iOS version and language;
  • your interaction with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic and performance data from the SDK itself.

CocoWash doesn’t share your photos, or what you decide about them, with Google. Google processes this data as an independent controller under its privacy policy: Google Ireland Limited in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and Google LLC elsewhere.

First, your consent

Before the first ad, if your region requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws), Google’s consent form appears. You can change your choice at any time in Filters › Support › Ad privacy.

Then, Apple’s tracking permission

When eligible and not previously decided, iOS asks for tracking permission. The IDFA is only used if you agree. If tracking is not authorized, CocoWash requests non-personalized ads. Google’s consent platform separately determines whether ads may be requested at all; if it does not allow requests, CocoWash does not request an ad. Non-personalized ads can still process device, IP, interaction and diagnostic data. Sorting your photos does not require accepting tracking. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

No ads

While “A year without ads” or “No ads, forever” is active, CocoWash stops requesting ads. More: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. On your iPhone, CocoWash receives Apple’s signed transaction for each purchase: which product it is, when it was bought and whether it was refunded. That tells the app whether to show ads and when “A year without ads” ends. The transaction isn’t sent to the controller or to any server. Your name, email and payment details stay with Apple.

Support emails

If you write to us, we use your address and what you tell us only to answer you and improve the app. Please don’t send personal photos.

What CocoWash doesn’t do

  • It has no user accounts and no servers of its own.
  • It includes no analytics or telemetry of its own.
  • It doesn’t sell or rent data for money.
  • It does not build profiles about health, beliefs or orientation. The media you choose to analyze locally can contain personal or sensitive content.

If you turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports with the developer, without data that identifies you.

Legal bases

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), its UK equivalent and Ecuador’s Organic Law on Personal Data Protection, each use relies on:

  • Sorting your library and purchases: providing the service you ask for. Your photos are analyzed on your iPhone and the controller never receives that data.
  • Place names: our legitimate interest in showing where you took each photo. You can object by turning off “Location”.
  • Personalized ads, and storing or reading information on your device for ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting earlier processing.
  • Non-personalized ads, measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the app for free and preventing abuse, and your consent where the law requires it.
  • Support emails: answering your request.

Transfers and retention

Google and Apple may process data outside your country, for example in the United States, with safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Support emails are received in Ecuador through an email provider that may store them in other countries.

Data stored on your iPhone is kept until you delete the app. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to answer the request and meet applicable legal obligations; you can request their deletion. Google keeps advertising data under its own policies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw your consent. Although library analysis stays on your iPhone, advertising providers and support email may process data outside it. In practice:

  • to erase everything the app stores, delete it;
  • for ads, use “Ad privacy” in the app, the iPhone Tracking setting or Google’s My Ad Center;
  • for anything else, write to cocowash@joaquinmino.work. We’ll answer within one month and may ask for just enough to confirm it’s you.

If you think we haven’t handled your request properly, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country: in the EU, your member state’s authority; in the UK, the ICO; in Ecuador, the Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales.

US residents

This section is the notice required by California’s CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, and by other state privacy laws such as those of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon.

What is collected and why

Through Google’s SDK on your device, these categories may be processed: identifiers (IDFA, only with your permission, and IP address), internet activity (interaction with ads), approximate (not precise) geolocation, device information and the advertising inferences Google may draw. They’re used to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, and they’re received by Google. CocoWash does not send the content of your photos or your library decisions to Google.

Sale and targeted advertising

We don’t sell data for money. Even so, letting Google use this data for personalized ads may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising (California) or “targeted advertising” in other states. You can opt out at any time in Filters › Support › Ad privacy and by not allowing tracking on your iPhone: ad requests then follow the updated consent status; ads may be non-personalized or unavailable. We don’t knowingly sell or share data of anyone under 16.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data is processed, to access, correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Using these rights doesn’t change the price or how the app works. You or an authorized agent can ask by writing to cocowash@joaquinmino.work. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to that email; if the appeal is also denied, you can contact your state attorney general.

Children

CocoWash isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect their data. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences. If you think a child has sent us data, write to us and we’ll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll publish the new version on this page with its date and provide any additional notice required by applicable law. For any question: cocowash@joaquinmino.work.
